Questions and support

Check the answers below, or send us your question and we will reply by email.

What does "starting at" mean?

It is the lowest rate for that service. Your final quote depends on the scope of work, and we confirm it before work begins.

How does payment work?

To confirm a booking we ask for a 50% advance through PayPal. The remaining balance is arranged in your quote.

What does "/mo" mean?

Marketing services marked /mo are monthly. Website, app and AI services show a starting price per project.

How soon will you reply?

We reply within one business day.

Do you work with clients outside my country?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide, and all prices are in US dollars.

How do I apply for a job?

Open the Careers section, choose a role and send the form with a link to your CV or portfolio.
